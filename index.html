From the classroom to the codebase

I studied History, Kiswahili and Arabic at the University of Dodoma, then spent two years teaching in Tanzanian secondary schools. Along the way I noticed how much time schools and small organisations lose to paper records and scattered information.

So I taught myself to build. I've made a school website, a student records system for a university association, a personal finance app and a digital-card platform. Each one started with a real person's problem, and that's still how I decide what to build.

Teaching gave me patience, clear communication and an instinct for explaining things simply. Those matter as much as code when you're building software for people who aren't technical.
